Support the David Lloyd Club at Overstone Leys

Have your say on the plans for a new David Lloyd health and wellness club on land at the corner of Sywell Road and Kettering Road, which would enhance the current amenity offering for Overstone Leys and the surrounding areas, creating over 100 permanent jobs and new opportunities for the local community.

This tool helps you write a short message of support for the David Lloyd club proposed at Overstone Leys, Northampton. Tick the parts of the scheme you support and a letter will be drafted for you below.

When you are happy with it, use the button to open the letter in your email app, ready to send to West Northamptonshire Council. You can edit and personalise the wording at any point before sending.

The application will be decided by the Strategic Planning Committee. The committee date is to be confirmed imminently.

Application reference: 2026/1770/MAF  •  West Northamptonshire Council
Planning committee: Strategic Planning Committee, date to be confirmed imminently

Did your email app not open? No problem. Your letter has been copied to your clipboard, so you can send it manually:

  1. Open your email app and start a new message.
  2. Send it to planning@westnorthants.gov.uk
  3. Use the subject: Support for planning application 2026/1770/MAF, David Lloyd Club, Overstone Leys
  4. Paste your letter into the message, review it to make sure it reflects your thoughts, edit as required, and send.
